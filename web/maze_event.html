1 · Maze

Use the Walls tool and click between two cells to add or remove a wall. Click the outer wall to open the entry and the exit (green dashes). Every change is sent to the robot. "Random maze" gives visitors a layout to build.

2 · Tools

Escape game

The entry is the green opening on the left of 0,0. Open one exit in the outer wall (click it). Put the robot outside, in front of the entry, facing in, then press Escape! It keeps its right hand on the wall until it is out. "Clear walls" keeps the entry.
